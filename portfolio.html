Sip Fisher

Sip Fisher is the cozy incremental game about sucking a lake up with a straw. It was a short student project with only three months development. It has grossed over $130,000 in revenue since it's release in August, 2025.

I was brought on to this project initially as a designer and the sole market researcher. I was the primary narrative designer, focused on the game's ending and the character writing for our loveable shopkeep. Post release, I became the project lead for content updates and bug fixes, working with a smaller team.
